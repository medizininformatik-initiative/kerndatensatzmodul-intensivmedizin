"Pressure difference ventilation" is a characteristic of the generic profile for [parameters of ventilation (Observation)](StructureDefinition-mii-pr-icu-parameter-von-beatmung.html). See there for more information regarding explanations of the items, or reference of the entries in the FHIR resource to the Logical Model. Pressure difference ventilation is the inspiratory airway pressure relative to PEEP or baseline airway pressure (delta Pinsp, e.g. Pmax minus PEEP), as defined for IEEE 11073 MDC_VENT_PRESS_AWAY_DELTA. The driving pressure (plateau pressure minus PEEP) is a different concept and must not be recorded with this profile.
